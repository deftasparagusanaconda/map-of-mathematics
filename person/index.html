Euclid systematised classical geometry through definitions, axioms and proofs
Archimedes developed influential results in geometry, mechanics and the method of exhaustion
Muhammad ibn Musa al-Khwarizmi systematised algebraic methods and gave algebra its name and algorithmic method
Isaac Newton co-developed calculus and applied mathematics to mechanics and gravitation
Leonhard Euler shaped large parts of analysis, number theory, graph theory and mathematical notation
Joseph Fourier developed Fourier analysis while studying heat flow
Augustin-Louis Cauchy helped establish rigorous foundations for calculus and complex analysis
Carl Friedrich Gauss made foundational contributions across number theory, geometry, analysis and astronomy
Bernhard Riemann reshaped geometry and analysis through manifolds, integration and complex functions
Évariste Galois connected polynomial equations with symmetry and founded Galois theory
Georg Cantor created set theory and developed the mathematical study of infinity
Henri Poincaré helped found topology and dynamical systems while contributing broadly across mathematics
David Hilbert influenced algebra, geometry, analysis and the formal foundations of mathematics
Emmy Noether transformed abstract algebra and connected symmetry with conservation laws
Srinivasa Ramanujan discovered striking formulas and identities in number theory and analysis
Kurt Gödel established profound limits on formal axiomatic systems
Alan Turing formalised computation and helped establish theoretical computer science
John von Neumann contributed widely to logic, analysis, quantum theory, computing and game theory
Paul Erdős produced an enormous collaborative body of work in combinatorics and number theory
Alexander Grothendieck recast algebraic geometry through schemes, categories and far-reaching structural methods
John Conway created influential ideas across algebra, geometry, number theory and recreational mathematics
Andrew Wiles proved Fermat's Last Theorem using ideas from elliptic curves and modular forms
Maryam Mirzakhani advanced the geometry and dynamics of moduli spaces and became the first woman awarded a Fields Medal
Terence Tao contributes broadly across analysis, number theory, combinatorics and partial differential equations
